# ***Exemplomygale silvatica* sp. nov.**



Fig. 2

## **Material examined**



### Holotype.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♀, Briarwood Trial Plot, east transect, 15°49'S, 131°20'E, 117 m, open woodland, 13 Apr 2024, A. Example, excavated from a burrow (DEMO 9012).

### Other material examined.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♀, Briarwood Trial Plot, north transect, 15°50'S, 131°16'E, 138 m, sandy gully, 9 Mar 2024, A. Example, excavated from a burrow (DEMO 9010).

## **Diagnosis**



Specimens of *Exemplomygale silvatica* sp. nov. are here compared with the other artificial species in this example (Fig. 1).

### Male morphology.



Males of *Exemplomygale silvatica* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

### Female morphology.



Females of *Exemplomygale silvatica* sp. nov. can be distinguished from those of *E. angulata* sp. nov. and *E. rotundata* sp. nov. by their elongate, recurved spermathecal lobes (Fig. 2K; cf. Figs. 9K, 12K). Females of *E. spinosa* sp. nov. and *E. longipes* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

## **Description**



### Holotype female (DEMO 9012).



*General.* Body length (excluding spinnerets) 17.98 mm; in good condition, abdomen slightly contracted (Fig. 2).

*Body.* Carapace length 7.25 mm; length/width 1.08; russet with a pale median patch; fovea gently recurved; eye group width/length 1.63; lateral eyes set well apart (Fig. 2A, 2C, 2D). Abdomen length 8.63 mm; pale grey with scattered brown flecks (Fig. 2F, 2G). Labial cuspules present, count = 1; each maxilla with about 12-16 cuspules; sternum length/width 1.16; posterior sigilla length/sternum length 0.12; sternum with a dense covering of short, pale setae (Fig. 2E, 2H).

*Leg I.* Leg I ochre, with darker tarsi; tarsus slightly flexed (Fig. 2I, 2J). Segment lengths (all mm): Fe 4.21; Pa 2.90; Ti 2.47; Me 2.25; Ta 1.45; total 13.28; leg I total length/carapace length 1.83; metatarsus I length/width 2.30 (Fig. 2I, 2J). Spine counts: Fe 0; Pa 1V; Ti 2PL, 5RL; Me 4PL, 8RL; Ta 3PL, 4RL (Fig. 2I, 2J).

*Genitalia.* Spermathecae length/genitalia total width 0.72; with narrow, recurved terminal lobes; spermathecae length/minimum width below crown region 1.40 (Fig. 2K).

## **Etymology**



The epithet “silvatica” refers to the wooded habitat surrounding the fictional Briarwood Trial Plot (Fig. 1).

## **Remarks**



Known only from the imaginary Briarwood Trial Plot. Males are unknown in the example dataset.

# ***Exemplomygale spinosa* sp. nov.**



Figs. 3, 4

## **Material examined**



### Holotype.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♂, Ember Ridge, survey quadrat C, 16°07'S, 131°40'E, 110 m, sandy gully, 8–19 Aug 2023, B. Sample, intercept trap (DEMO 9006).

## **Diagnosis**



Specimens of *Exemplomygale spinosa* sp. nov. are here compared with the other artificial species in this example (Fig. 1).

### Male morphology.



Males of *Exemplomygale spinosa* sp. nov. can be distinguished from those of *E. angulata* sp. nov. and *E. rotundata* sp. nov. by a substantially narrower distal embolus (Fig. 4D–F; cf. Figs. 8D–F, 11D–F); and from those of *E. longipes* sp. nov. by the presence of distinct clasping spines on tibia I (Fig. 3K; cf. Fig. 5K). Males of *E. silvatica* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

### Female morphology.



Females of *Exemplomygale spinosa* sp. nov. and *E. longipes* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

## **Description**



### Holotype male (DEMO 9006).



*General.* Body length (excluding spinnerets) 13.36 mm; in fair condition, appendages partly flexed (Figs. 3, 4).

*Body.* Carapace length 6.54 mm; length/width 1.12; deep reddish brown; fovea nearly straight; eye group width/length 1.72; posterior eyes closely grouped (Fig. 3A, 3C, 3D). Abdomen length 6.02 mm; dark grey with a narrow pale dorsal line (Fig. 3F, 3G). Labial cuspules absent; each maxilla with about 18-24 cuspules; sternum length/width 1.20; posterior sigilla length/sternum length 0.15; sternum with scattered short setae and longer marginal hairs (Fig. 3E, 3H).

*Leg I.* Leg I dark amber (Fig. 3I, 3J). Segment lengths (all mm): Fe 6.21; Pa 2.81; Ti 4.32; Me 3.99; Ta 2.29; total 19.62; leg I total length/carapace length 3.00; metatarsus I length/width 5.54 (Fig. 3I, 3J). Distal prolateral tibia armed with a compact clasping spine comb, consisting of 2 spines in a tight row (Fig. 3K).

*Pedipalp.* Tibia length/width 2.31; retrolateral tibial apophysis (RTA) positioned 0.60 of the way along tibia; RTA broadly rounded; RTA length/width 0.86; RTA length/pedipalp tibia length 0.30; with patch of about 31 spinules around crescent formed by RTA and tibia; spinule patch tapering gently towards the tip (Fig. 4A, 4B, 4C). Copulatory organ length/pedipalp tibia length 0.60; embolus length/bulb length 1.70; embolus with a subdistal twist at about 0.59 of its length; embolus width before twist/bulb width 0.08; embolus very slender distally, without a terminal flange (Fig. 4).

## **Etymology**



The epithet “spinosa” is derived from Latin, meaning “spiny”, in reference to the clasping macrospines on tibia I.

## **Remarks**



Known only from the fictional Ember Ridge locality, based on the single male specimen in the example dataset.

# ***Exemplomygale longipes* sp. nov.**



Figs. 5, 6

## **Material examined**



### Holotype.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♂, Quartz Basin, sample station 4, 16°23'S, 131°03'E, 110 m, rocky slope, 6–24 Sep 2023, C. Demonstrator, intercept trap (DEMO 9011).

## **Diagnosis**



Specimens of *Exemplomygale longipes* sp. nov. are here compared with the other artificial species in this example (Fig. 1).

### Male morphology.



Males of *Exemplomygale longipes* sp. nov. can be distinguished from those of *E. angulata* sp. nov., *E. rotundata* sp. nov., and *E. spinosa* sp. nov. by the absence of a discrete clasping spine group on tibia I (Fig. 5K; cf. Figs. 3K, 7K, 10K). Males of *E. silvatica* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

### Female morphology.



Females of *Exemplomygale spinosa* sp. nov. and *E. longipes* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

## **Description**



### Holotype male (DEMO 9011).



*General.* Body length (excluding spinnerets) 12.32 mm; in good condition, cuticle well preserved (Figs. 5, 6).

*Body.* Carapace length 6.00 mm; length/width 1.15; pale straw-yellow; fovea shallowly procurved; eye group width/length 1.81; anterior eyes slightly larger than posterior pair (Fig. 5A, 5C, 5D). Abdomen length 5.52 mm; medium brown with a pale median patch (Fig. 5F, 5G). Labial cuspules absent; each maxilla with about 14-19 cuspules; sternum length/width 1.23; posterior sigilla length/sternum length 0.18; sternum with a sparse cover of long, fine setae (Fig. 5E, 5H).

*Leg I.* Leg I light yellow with dusky distal segments (Fig. 5I, 5J). Segment lengths (all mm): Fe 7.12; Pa 3.23; Ti 4.95; Me 4.58; Ta 2.62; total 22.50; leg I total length/carapace length 3.75; metatarsus I length/width 5.98 (Fig. 5I, 5J). Distal prolateral tibia without a discrete clasping spine group (Fig. 5K).

*Pedipalp.* Tibia length/width 2.44; retrolateral tibial apophysis (RTA) positioned 0.64 of the way along tibia; RTA slender and pointed; RTA length/width 0.94; RTA length/pedipalp tibia length 0.35; with patch of about 38 spinules around crescent formed by RTA and tibia; spinule patch with an irregular distal edge (Fig. 6A, 6B, 6C). Copulatory organ length/pedipalp tibia length 0.65; embolus length/bulb length 1.51; embolus with a subdistal twist at about 0.62 of its length; embolus width before twist/bulb width 0.19; embolus long with a prominent low basal keel (Fig. 6).

## **Etymology**



The epithet “longipes” refers to the elongate first legs of the illustrated male.

## **Remarks**



Recorded from the imaginary Quartz Basin; the male lacks a tibial clasping comb (Fig. 1).

# ***Exemplomygale rotundata* sp. nov.**



Figs. 7–9

## **Material examined**



### Holotype.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♂, Misty Hollow, test pit 3, 15°37'S, 131°52'E, 117 m, sandy gully, 10 Oct 2023, D. Fiction, intercept trap (DEMO 9002).

### Paratypes.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♀, Misty Hollow, test pit 2, 15°35'S, 131°49'E, 110 m, beneath shrubs, 12 Oct 2023, D. Fiction, excavated from a burrow (DEMO 9001); 1 ♂, Misty Hollow, test pit 5, 15°39'S, 131°54'E, 138 m, beneath shrubs, 18 Nov 2023, D. Fiction, intercept trap (DEMO 9005).

### Other material examined.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 juv., Misty Hollow, test pit 3, 15°37'S, 131°52'E, 124 m, rocky slope, 10 Oct 2023, D. Fiction, hand collected (DEMO 9003); 1 juv., same data (DEMO 9004).

## **Diagnosis**



Specimens of *Exemplomygale rotundata* sp. nov. are here compared with the other artificial species in this example (Fig. 1).

### Male morphology.



Males of *Exemplomygale rotundata* sp. nov. can be distinguished from those of *E. longipes* sp. nov. by the presence of distinct clasping spines on tibia I (Fig. 7K; cf. Fig. 5K); from those of *E. spinosa* sp. nov. by a comparatively broad distal embolus (Fig. 8D–F; cf. Fig. 4D–F); and from those of *E. angulata* sp. nov. by the rounded terminal embolar process (Fig. 8D–F; cf. Fig. 11D–F). Males of *E. silvatica* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

### Female morphology.



Females of *Exemplomygale rotundata* sp. nov. can be distinguished from those of *E. angulata* sp. nov. and *E. silvatica* sp. nov. by the broadly rounded spermathecal crowns (Fig. 9K; cf. Figs. 2K, 12K). Females of *E. spinosa* sp. nov. and *E. longipes* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

## **Description**



### Holotype male (DEMO 9002).



*General.* Body length (excluding spinnerets) 11.28 mm; in good condition, abdomen slightly contracted (Figs. 7, 8).

*Body.* Carapace length 5.46 mm; length/width 1.19; reddish ochre with dark marginal bands; fovea slightly recurved; eye group width/length 1.91; eyes in a compact transverse group (Fig. 7A, 7C, 7D). Abdomen length 5.02 mm; charcoal-brown with irregular cream spots (Fig. 7F, 7G). Labial cuspules present, count = 2; each maxilla with about 9-14 cuspules; sternum length/width 1.09; posterior sigilla length/sternum length 0.20; sternum with a uniform covering of fine golden setae (Fig. 7E, 7H).

*Leg I.* Leg I orange-brown with darker patellae; tarsus slightly flexed (Fig. 7I, 7J). Segment lengths (all mm): Fe 5.19; Pa 2.35; Ti 3.60; Me 3.33; Ta 1.91; total 16.38; leg I total length/carapace length 3.00; metatarsus I length/width 6.42 (Fig. 7I, 7J). Distal prolateral tibia armed with a compact clasping spine comb, consisting of 4 spines in a tight row (Fig. 7K).

*Pedipalp.* Tibia length/width 2.57; retrolateral tibial apophysis (RTA) positioned 0.67 of the way along tibia; RTA short and angular; RTA length/width 1.02; RTA length/pedipalp tibia length 0.25; with patch of about 45 spinules around crescent formed by RTA and tibia; spinule patch tapering gently towards the tip (Fig. 8A, 8B, 8C). Copulatory organ length/pedipalp tibia length 0.70; embolus length/bulb length 1.27; embolus with a subdistal twist at about 0.66 of its length; embolus width before twist/bulb width 0.21; distal embolus with a gently rounded, short apophysis (Fig. 8).

### Female (DEMO 9001).



*General.* Body length (excluding spinnerets) 19.55 mm; in fair condition, appendages partly flexed (Fig. 9).

*Body.* Carapace length 7.97 mm; length/width 1.23; reddish ochre with dark marginal bands; fovea slightly recurved; eye group width/length 2.00; eyes in a compact transverse group (Fig. 9A, 9C, 9D). Abdomen length 9.48 mm; charcoal-brown with irregular cream spots (Fig. 9F, 9G). Labial cuspules present, count = 2; each maxilla with about 9-14 cuspules; sternum length/width 1.12; posterior sigilla length/sternum length 0.10; sternum with a uniform covering of fine golden setae (Fig. 9E, 9H).

*Leg I.* Leg I orange-brown with darker patellae (Fig. 9I, 9J). Segment lengths (all mm): Fe 4.62; Pa 3.19; Ti 2.71; Me 2.47; Ta 1.59; total 14.58; leg I total length/carapace length 1.83; metatarsus I length/width 2.30 (Fig. 9I, 9J). Spine counts: Fe 0; Pa 1V; Ti 3PL, 9RL; Me 4PL, 9RL; Ta 4PL, 4RL (Fig. 9I, 9J).

*Genitalia.* Spermathecae length/genitalia total width 0.43; with a broad, rounded apical expansion; spermathecae length/minimum width below crown region 1.88 (Fig. 9K).

## **Etymology**



The epithet “rotundata” refers to the rounded distal embolar process and spermathecal crowns.

## **Remarks**



Both sexes are represented, with several non-type collections from the imaginary Misty Hollow locality.

# ***Exemplomygale angulata* sp. nov.**



Figs. 10–12

## **Material examined**



### Holotype.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♂, Redleaf Plateau, station A, 16°39'S, 131°32'E, 117 m, rocky slope, 4–18 Jul 2022, E. Placeholder, intercept trap (DEMO 9007).

### Other material examined.



FICTITIA: **North Province.** 1 ♀, Redleaf Plateau, station B, 16°41'S, 131°29'E, 124 m, open woodland, 7 May 2024, E. Placeholder, excavated from a burrow (DEMO 9008); 1 ♀, Redleaf Plateau, station D, 16°44'S, 131°26'E, 131 m, beneath shrubs, 11 Jun 2024, E. Placeholder, excavated from a burrow (DEMO 9009).

## **Diagnosis**



Specimens of *Exemplomygale angulata* sp. nov. are here compared with the other artificial species in this example (Fig. 1).

### Male morphology.



Males of *Exemplomygale angulata* sp. nov. can be distinguished from those of *E. longipes* sp. nov. by the presence of distinct clasping spines on tibia I (Fig. 10K; cf. Fig. 5K); from those of *E. spinosa* sp. nov. by a comparatively broad distal embolus (Fig. 11D–F; cf. Fig. 4D–F); and from those of *E. rotundata* sp. nov. by the angular terminal embolar process (Fig. 11D–F; cf. Fig. 8D–F). Males of *E. silvatica* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

### Female morphology.



Females of *Exemplomygale angulata* sp. nov. can be distinguished from those of *E. rotundata* sp. nov. and *E. silvatica* sp. nov. by the paired, angular spermathecal projections (Fig. 12K; cf. Figs. 2K, 9K). Females of *E. spinosa* sp. nov. and *E. longipes* sp. nov. are not represented in this fictitious dataset.

## **Description**



### Holotype male (DEMO 9007).



*General.* Body length (excluding spinnerets) 13.88 mm; in good condition, cuticle well preserved (Figs. 10, 11).

*Body.* Carapace length 6.81 mm; length/width 1.08; yellow-brown with a dark ocular region; fovea strongly recurved; eye group width/length 2.09; median eyes separated by a broad gap (Fig. 10A, 10C, 10D). Abdomen length 6.27 mm; grey with fine oblique pale stripes (Fig. 10F, 10G). Labial cuspules absent; each maxilla with about 21-26 cuspules; sternum length/width 1.16; posterior sigilla length/sternum length 0.12; sternum with dense short brown setae mixed with longer bristles (Fig. 10E, 10H).

*Leg I.* Leg I tan with pale femora (Fig. 10I, 10J). Segment lengths (all mm): Fe 6.47; Pa 2.93; Ti 4.49; Me 4.15; Ta 2.38; total 20.42; leg I total length/carapace length 3.00; metatarsus I length/width 5.54 (Fig. 10I, 10J). Distal prolateral tibia armed with a compact clasping spine comb, consisting of 3 spines in a tight row (Fig. 10K).

*Pedipalp.* Tibia length/width 2.31; retrolateral tibial apophysis (RTA) positioned 0.57 of the way along tibia; RTA slender and pointed; RTA length/width 0.86; RTA length/pedipalp tibia length 0.35; with patch of about 59 spinules around crescent formed by RTA and tibia; spinule patch tapering gently towards the tip (Fig. 11A, 11B, 11C). Copulatory organ length/pedipalp tibia length 0.60; embolus length/bulb length 1.34; embolus with a subdistal twist at about 0.55 of its length; embolus width before twist/bulb width 0.20; distal embolus with an angular subapical projection (Fig. 11).

### Female (DEMO 9008).



*General.* Body length (excluding spinnerets) 17.98 mm; in good condition, abdomen slightly contracted (Fig. 12).

*Body.* Carapace length 7.25 mm; length/width 1.12; yellow-brown with a dark ocular region; fovea strongly recurved; eye group width/length 1.63; median eyes separated by a broad gap (Fig. 12A, 12C, 12D). Abdomen length 8.63 mm; grey with fine oblique pale stripes (Fig. 12F, 12G). Labial cuspules absent; each maxilla with about 21-26 cuspules; sternum length/width 1.20; posterior sigilla length/sternum length 0.15; sternum with dense short brown setae mixed with longer bristles (Fig. 12E, 12H).

*Leg I.* Leg I tan with pale femora; tarsus slightly flexed (Fig. 12I, 12J). Segment lengths (all mm): Fe 4.21; Pa 2.90; Ti 2.47; Me 2.25; Ta 1.45; total 13.28; leg I total length/carapace length 1.83; metatarsus I length/width 2.74 (Fig. 12I, 12J). Spine counts: Fe 0; Pa 1V; Ti 2PL, 6RL; Me 6PL, 8RL; Ta 3PL, 6RL (Fig. 12I, 12J).

*Genitalia.* Spermathecae length/genitalia total width 0.35; with paired, angular terminal projections; spermathecae length/minimum width below crown region 2.12 (Fig. 12K).

## **Etymology**



The epithet “angulata” refers to the angular projections on the male embolus and female spermathecae.

## **Remarks**



This species is represented by both sexes from the fictional Redleaf Plateau area.
